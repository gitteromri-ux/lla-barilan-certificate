Assess market demand, test messages and audiences, and evaluate acquiring prospective students through paid digital channels. US audience.

3.2

Decisions built into the page.

Brand pattern

Mirrors eTeacher’s university schools

Rosen School of Hebrew and the Israel Institute of Biblical Studies lead with the school brand, show “Courses offered in Partnership with the Hebrew University of Jerusalem” with the HUJI logo, and keep eTeacher in the footer as operator. Here: LLA plus the Bar-Ilan logo in the header, the proposal’s issued-through wording, and “operated by eTeacher Group” in the footer. No “by eTeacher Group” headline.

Credibility rules

Nothing invented

No price, review, rating, testimonial, partner logo or quote is invented. External statistics (WHO, U.S. Census, peer-reviewed studies) are cited with links and labeled as population-level findings. APHEA appears only as the gold line, in the credibility section.

Methodology

The Evidence-to-Practice Model

A proprietary three-layer model (evidence, method, judgment) built only from the proposal’s ten goals, six courses and assessment weighting, with a competency × assessment matrix. Presented as the program’s methodology, not as a study.

Conversion

Appendix A lead form

Four steps: contact, background, interest and pathway, then Appendix A’s two qualifying questions (enrollment timeline, primary goal), OTP verification and a thank-you state. UTMs and click IDs are captured. Everything routes to eTeacher’s CRM.

Compliance

Consent first

Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied. GA4, Google Ads and Meta load only after consent, and only once real IDs replace the placeholders. Pages are set to noindex until approvals are complete.

Footage

Swap-ready video

Every video slot uses free-licence 4K stock (or fitting LLA footage) encoded for the web, with posters. footage/SHOTLIST.md maps each slot to an exact Artlist/Artgrid replacement brief.

Built from the materials pack in /materials. Landing page: site/ · Insights: site/blog/ · Footage shotlist: footage/SHOTLIST.md
