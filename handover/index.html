Appendix · Full scope of work

Marketing phases in detail

Two phases: build everything, then test demand in market.

Phase 1 · up to 4 weeks

Planning & preparation

Audience research, messaging development, marketing content and creative production, landing pages and campaign setup.

1.1

Audience research, messaging & creative direction

  • Target audience personas and messaging framework
  • Key marketing messages and value propositions
  • Creative direction and graphic design for campaign materials
  • Adapting messaging and creative assets based on campaign performance
1.2

Paid media assets & campaign preparation

  • Up to 2 promotional videos (~90 seconds), up to 8 format adaptations
  • 4 static feed ads, stories/reels and 2 carousels
  • 2 landing pages and 3 creative-rich lead forms with OTP
  • 100+ Google Search ad variations
  • Creative production, campaign setup, landing page programming, design and motion assets
1.3

Course collateral & supporting content

  • 3 video test ads (~90 seconds)
  • 8 blog posts with one-page summaries and cover designs
  • Optional: 2 lead magnets, health education materials and cheat sheets
Phase 2 · 30 days

Demand validation

Campaign launch, audience and messaging testing, performance measurement and analysis, including about 3 weeks of active optimization.

2.1

Demand validation & optimization

  • Campaigns across Meta and Google Search
  • Testing audiences, messages and creative approaches
  • Optimizing ads, targeting and media allocation
  • Monitoring leads, acquisition costs and conversion indicators
  • Performance reporting and final assessment of market demand
2.2

Media plan

  • Meta: audience testing, video and static ads, lead generation and retargeting
  • Google Search: campaigns targeting relevant demand, measuring acquisition performance
  • Landing pages & lead forms: conversion testing, lead capture and measurement
  • Budget can shift between channels during validation, within the approved total
2.3

Lead definition

  • US audience; contact details given after a high-level program overview
  • Two qualifying questions: expected enrollment timing, and main goal
  • Showing price or a price range: recommended, not mandatory
Bottom line

Phase 1 builds every asset; Phase 2 tests it on Meta and Google and reports real cost per lead.
