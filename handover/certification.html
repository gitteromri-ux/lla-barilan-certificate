01 · Short answer

No single board on either side

United States

One national exam
No certificate is mandatory. “Longevity instructor” is not a licensed profession in the US, and no universally recognized longevity coaching certification exists. People hold credentials borrowed from neighboring fields.

Europe

Country by country
Europe has no single board. It works country by country, with the UK the most organized market. France, Italy, Spain and the Nordics not yet checked individually.

Ranking order is our judgment; the facts in each item are sourced. Prices change with promotions: confirm on each provider’s site before using in a deck.

02 · Ranked by weight, side by side

The certificates

United States

5 tiers
  1. 01

    NBC-HWC · National Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach

    The closest thing to a real board for coaches

    • Exam run in partnership with the National Board of Medical Examiners
    • Requires graduating from an NBHWC Approved Training Program, 50 logged coaching sessions, and an associate degree or higher
    • The credential clinics, insurers and employers look for
  2. 02

    Lifestyle medicine · DipABLM / DipACLM

    The clinical tier

    • ABLM certifies US physicians; ACLM certifies other eligible health professionals
    • Non-physician route requires a master’s or doctorate in a health discipline
    • Coaches can’t hold it. Their route: an NBC-HWC or Certified Wellcoach who finishes “Lifestyle Medicine for Coaches” gets the Lifestyle Medicine Coach Certificate
    • Gives medical credibility and an ACLM directory listing
  3. 03

    NCCA-accredited fitness certification · ACE, NASM, ACSM, NSCA

    The standard for anyone who trains bodies

    • NCCA accreditation is the quality mark gyms and insurers check
    • Gives the right to be hired to lead exercise, plus liability insurance
  4. 04

    Aging specialty add-ons · Senior Fitness / Functional Aging Specialist

    Niche label

    • Short courses stacked on a trainer certification, focused on older adults
    • On their own they carry little weight
  5. 05

    Branded “Certified Longevity Coach” programs

    Commercial certificates

    • Each from its own school, often tied to a specific commercial program
    • A title and a niche; no outside body stands behind them

Europe

6 tiers
  1. 01

    UKIHCA-approved Level 5 health coaching diploma

    UK, accepted internationally

    • Gives a professional register and employability: graduates can register as a UKIHCA Registered Health Coach
    • £3,599 – £4,980 · 6–12 months · 600–740 learning hours
    • Health Coaches Academy: £4,980, 12 months, 600 hours
    • CNM: 6–12 months, 740 hours, price not shown
    • Active Health Group: £3,599, lessons over 12 months
    • IINH (Ireland): 10 months online, price not shown
  2. 02

    PCI-accredited health coaching course

    The UK’s NHS route

    • Mandatory 4-day accredited training for NHS health and wellbeing coaches
    • 4 days, about 25 live hours
    • Cost: not verified
  3. 03

    EREPS registration

    Pan-European fitness register

    • Portability: an EQF Level 4 trainer is recognized by employers across Europe
    • UK example: Level 2 + 3 packages £1,200 – £2,500, about 3–6 months
    • Providers accredited by EuropeActive; in the UK, CIMSPA-endorsed schools such as OriGym
  4. 04

    Lifestyle medicine · IBLM (with BSLM in the UK)

    Clinicians only

    • Medical-grade authority; non-physicians need a master’s or doctorate in a health field
    • UK exam enrolment £1,069 – £1,399, plus prerequisite courses
    • Shorter option: ELMO’s eight-week online certificate
  5. 05

    Germany · ZPP prevention course certification

    Unlocks insurer funding

    • Health insurers cover a prevention course only once ZPP has certified it
    • Certifies a course and its leader; the leader needs a matching state-recognized or university degree
    • Review is free for providers
  6. 06

    Geneva College of Longevity Science

    Closest competitor

    • A longevity title from a Swiss institution, with tracks for physicians, physiotherapists, nutrition experts and fitness coaches
    • Executive Master 25,000 CHF · Certified Longevity Physician 6,600 CHF · Certified Longevity Physiotherapist 2,500 CHF
    • Master’s: two semesters by distance learning; professional certificates described as three-month hybrid programs (UAE partnership)
03 · Scope

The line they cannot cross (US)

  • A certificate is not a license.
  • Most states require no license to work as a health coach.
  • Detailed nutrition plans or medical advice can count as practicing without a license.
  • Diagnosing, treating and prescribing stay with licensed clinicians.

What matters most to people choosing a program

  1. 01

    A credential someone else recognizes

    Does it lead to a board exam, or is it only the school’s own paper?

  2. 02

    The name on the diploma

    A university certificate beats a private academy.

  3. 03

    Clear scope

    What they are allowed to do with clients, and what they are not.

  4. 04

    Supervised practice

    Real coaching hours, not only lectures.

  5. 05

    A way to earn

    Client-getting, insurance, a directory or referral network.

05 · Implication

What this means for the Bar-Ilan program

United States

NBHWC
  • Strongest claim available: NBHWC Approved Training Program status, making graduates eligible for the US board exam.
  • Approved programs exist internationally, so an Israeli university is not excluded in principle.
  • Not yet checked: whether the planned curriculum meets the approval standards.
  • Without it, the program sits in tier 5 with a university name on it, still a real advantage over private schools.

Europe

UKIHCA
  • 12 months is the European norm, not the ceiling: top UK diplomas run 12 months, 600–740 hours, £3,600 – £5,000.
  • The European counterpart to NBHWC approval is UKIHCA approval. A program can hold both: FMCA is approved by NBHWC and UKIHCA.
  • Not yet checked: whether the curriculum would qualify.
  • Geneva College of Longevity Science is the name to position against: it already sells longevity certificates to fitness coaches and clinicians.
