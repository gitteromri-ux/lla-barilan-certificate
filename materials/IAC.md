**Tablecloth Design Guidelines – Rosen School of Hebrew (by eTeacher) | IAC Summit**
- **Purpose: Create a branded tablecloth for a conference booth table. The logo must be clearly visible on the front-facing side (the side attendees see when approaching the table).**
- **Table size (Full Booth): 6 ft (W) × 30 in (D).**
- **Logo placement (Front-facing):**
- **Place the logo on the front panel / front drop of the tablecloth (the vertical fabric that hangs down on the attendee-facing side).**
- **Center the logo horizontally on the front panel.**
- **Position the logo vertically in the middle of the front panel (not too close to the top edge, not too close to the bottom hem).**
- **Branding text: Use the lockup: “Rosen School of Hebrew” with the sub-line “by eTeacher” (as per the official brand/logo files).**
- **Safe margins:**
- **Keep at least 3–4 inches of padding from the left/right edges.**
- **Keep at least 3 inches from the bottom hem to avoid the logo being cut off or folded.**
- **Readability requirements:**
- **The logo should be large enough to be readable from 2–3 meters / 6–10 feet away.**
- **Avoid placing key elements where table legs or folds are likely to occur.**
- **Files & output:**
- **Deliver as print-ready PDF (CMYK, 300 DPI) + source file (AI/PSD).**
- **Use vector logo files when possible.**
