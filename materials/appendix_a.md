Appendix A – Pre-Pilot Action Plan, Marketing Plan & Budget
1. Objective
The Pre-Pilot is designed to assess market demand for the proposed Longevity education offering, test key marketing messages and target audiences, and evaluate the potential for acquiring prospective students through paid digital marketing channels.

The target audience will be based in the United States.
A lead is defined as a prospect who has provided their contact details after reviewing a high-level overview of the program. Displaying the program's pricing or price range is recommended but not mandatory. Each lead must answer two qualifying questions:
1.	Enrollment timeline: When do they expect to enroll?
2.	Objective: What is their primary goal or motivation for joining the program?

The Pre-Pilot will include marketing planning, content and creative development, campaign execution, and a 30-day demand validation process.
2. Action Plan and Timeline
The Pre-Pilot will be implemented in two consecutive phases:
Phase	Timeline	Main Activities
Phase 1 – Planning & Preparation	Up to 4 weeks	Audience research, messaging development, marketing content and creative production, landing pages and campaign setup
Phase 2 – Demand Validation	30 days	Campaign launch, audience and messaging testing, performance measurement and analysis, including 3 weeks of active campaign optimization
The Demand Validation phase will include an initial campaign launch and data collection period, followed by approximately three weeks of campaign optimization and continued testing.
3. Scope of Work
A. Audience Research, Messaging & Creative Direction
•	Target audience personas and messaging framework
•	Development of key marketing messages and value propositions
•	Creative direction and graphic design for campaign materials
•	Adaptation of messaging and creative assets based on campaign performance
B. Paid Media Assets & Campaign Preparation
•	Up to 2 promotional videos (approximately 90 seconds),...p to 8 format adaptations
•	4 static feed advertisements, stories/reels and 2 carousels
•	2 landing pages and 3 creative-rich lead generation forms, including OTP functionality
•	100+ Google Search advertising variations
•	Creative production, campaign setup, landing page programming, design and motion assets
C. Course Collateral & Supporting Content
•	3 video test advertisements (approximately 90 seconds)
•	8 blog posts, including one-page summaries and cover designs
•	Optional 2 lead magnets, including health-related educational materials and cheat sheets
D. Demand Validation & Campaign Optimization
•	Campaign execution across Meta and Google Search
•	Testing of audiences, marketing messages and creative approaches
•	Optimization of advertising assets, targeting and media allocation
•	Monitoring of lead generation, acquisition costs and conversion indicators
•	Campaign performance reporting and final assessment of market demand
The scope above represents the planned activities and deliverables. Specific creative outputs and quantities may be reasonably adjusted based on implementation needs and campaign learnings, provided that the activities remain within the Approved Plan and its budget.
4. Media Plan
The primary objective of paid media activity is to validate demand and measure the ability to generate qualified leads for the proposed Longevity offering.
Channel	Planned Activity
Meta	Audience testing, video and static advertisements, lead generation campaigns and retargeting
Google Search	Search campaigns targeting relevant demand and measuring acquisition performance
Landing Pages & Lead Forms	Conversion testing, lead capture and measurement
Media expenditure will be allocated between the approved channels based on campaign performance. eTeacher may adjust channel allocations, targeting, creatives and campaign settings during the Demand Validation phase without additional approval, provided that the total approved budget is not exceeded.
