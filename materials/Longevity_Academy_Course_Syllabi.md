*Designed for active adults in their 40s, 50s, and 60s who train seriously but are fighting hormonal decline, slower recovery, and rising injury risk. This course pairs exercise science with longevity medicine to extend your performance window by a decade or more.*

## **Session-by-Session Curriculum**


*A deep-dive into metabolic health for adults who want to go beyond the flagship course. Built for graduates who got their blood work back and want to act on it — and for anyone with pre-diabetes, elevated ApoB, metabolic syndrome, or unexplained fatigue who wants a structured protocol backed by their own data.*

## **Session-by-Session Curriculum**


*Cognitive decline is one of the most feared consequences of aging — and one of the least addressed in standard care. This course applies the same Medicine 3.0 framework to the brain: upstream biomarkers, modifiable risk factors, and a personal neuroprotection protocol built from your own data.*

## **Session-by-Session Curriculum**


*The flagship course is necessarily gender-neutral. But women in their 40s and 50s navigating perimenopause face physiology that changes the entire longevity equation. This course builds a women-specific protocol around the hormonal transition — covering metabolism, cardiovascular risk, bone density, sleep, mental health, and the HRT evidence that has fundamentally shifted in the last decade.*

## **Session-by-Session Curriculum**


*The capstone course for graduates of any Longevity Life Academy program. This is where science, data, and personalized protocol design converge. You will run a comprehensive biomarker battery — including epigenetic biological age testing — and spend 18 sessions learning to interpret, act on, and retest every number. This is not education about longevity science. It is the application of longevity science to your specific biology.*
