03 · Curriculum

Six courses. One progressive journey.

Four stages, from the science of longevity to supervised practice. A professional-practice strand runs through every course.

01
Stage 01 · Course 1

The Science of Longevity: What Actually Extends Life?

Examine the scientific foundations of longevity and healthy aging: major biological processes, population-level findings, risk and protective factors, and the distinction between established evidence and emerging or unsupported longevity claims.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
02
Stage 02 · Course 2

Food as Medicine: Nutrition for Lifelong Health

Explore the relationship between food, chronic disease prevention, functional health and healthy aging, with evidence-based nutritional principles applied in different personal, cultural and socioeconomic contexts. Health inequities in access to food and food culture are central.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
03
Stage 02 · Course 3

Habits That Shape Health: Behavior Change and Longevity

Examine how habits are formed, maintained and changed. Learn practical behavior-change tools and consider how individual choices are shaped by family, community, environment and wider social systems, including ingrained inequality in social structures.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
04
Stage 02 · Course 4

Sleep, Stress, and Healthy Aging

Study the roles of sleep, stress, recovery and physiological regulation in lifelong health. Assess common barriers to healthy routines and develop realistic strategies for improving sleep and stress management.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
05
Stage 03 · Course 5

Purpose, Meaning, and Emotional Wellbeing

Explore the relationship between emotional wellbeing, purpose, resilience, identity, life transitions and healthy aging, with reflective tools that can be applied personally or in professional and community settings.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
06
Stage 03 · Course 6

Social Connection and Longevity

Examine social connection as a central dimension of health and longevity: loneliness, belonging, family and community relationships, social participation, and the development of supportive environments across the life course.

4 live lessons · 90 min each
04
Stage 04 · Practicum & capstone

Supervised practice and capstone

Bring it together in a supervised practice case and a capstone portfolio built across the program, with three practicum and capstone clinics and a final presentation and oral assessment.

3 clinics · 2 final assessment sessions

08 · FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Everything here comes from the program proposal. Anything not yet approved is marked as such.

Is this a university degree?

No. It is a structured professional-training and certification program. Participants who complete all requirements receive the Professional Certificate in Longevity and Lifelong Wellbeing Advisory Practice, issued through Bar-Ilan Akadima and Health 360, in partnership with Longevity Life Academy, subject to final institutional approval, approved nomenclature and branding requirements.

What will the certificate allow me to do, and what not?

Graduates who meet all academic, practical, assessment and conduct requirements will be eligible to use the proposed designation Certified Longevity and Lifelong Wellbeing Advisor, subject to final institutional approval. The certificate adds a defined advisory specialization to your existing professional role, or allows you to take on an independent coaching or advisory practice. It does not confer a medical, nutritional, psychological or other regulated clinical license. Graduates may not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, alter medication or provide services reserved for licensed professionals unless separately qualified.

What are the admission requirements?

Recommended admission requirements for the certification pathway include a relevant academic degree or recognized professional qualification. Applicants with substantial relevant experience may be admitted following individual review. All certification participants commit to the attendance, assessment, practicum and professional-conduct requirements and acknowledge the program’s scope of practice.

How is it delivered, and how much time does it take?

The program is delivered live online. It includes 30 synchronous lessons of 90 minutes (45 clock hours, or 60 academic hours, of live instruction): 24 lessons across the six courses, one orientation and professional-scope lesson, three practicum and capstone clinics and two final assessment sessions. Together with about 30 hours of guided independent learning and about 25 hours of practicum and capstone work, the total is about 100 clock hours. It runs over 30 teaching weeks and is normally completed in about eight months, excluding institutional breaks.

Who teaches the program?

Synchronous online instruction is delivered by trained instructors from the eTeacher team, who receive program-specific training and teaching materials. Core content is developed or approved by Health 360 subject-matter experts, and an academic director appointed or approved by Bar-Ilan Akadima and Health 360 holds responsibility for the syllabus, professional scope, assessment standards and certification decisions.

How is my work assessed?

Throughout the program rather than through attendance alone. Assessment includes participation, short knowledge checks, applied and case-based assignments, an observed or recorded skills assessment, a supervised practice case, a progressively developed capstone portfolio and a final presentation and oral assessment. To be certified you attend at least 85% of synchronous sessions and all required clinics, complete all assignments, achieve at least 70% in each course and 75% overall, complete the practice case, have your portfolio approved and pass the final oral assessment.

Can I take a single course?

Yes. Standalone enrichment courses are open to members of the general public and to professionals seeking focused continuing education, without the practicum, capstone portfolio or final professional assessment. Successful completion may lead to a course-specific certificate of completion or participation, subject to Bar-Ilan Akadima policy. It does not confer the professional certificate or the graduate designation.
