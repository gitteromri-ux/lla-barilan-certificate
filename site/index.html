01About the program

A professional training sequence, not a collection of courses.

A structured professional-training and certification program for professionals who want to expand their existing field of practice through a specialization in longevity and wellbeing.

It prepares participants to apply evidence-informed principles with individuals, groups, workplaces and communities, within a clearly defined non-clinical advisory scope.

The program combines academic knowledge from Bar-Ilan University and Health 360 with accessible instruction, practical tools, case-based learning and real-world application. Each course builds on the knowledge and tools introduced in the one before, and participants develop a supervised practice case and a capstone portfolio progressively throughout the program.

“Participants progress from foundational scientific knowledge to evidence appraisal, practical skills, supervised application, and demonstration of competence.”From the program proposal

03Our methodology

The Evidence-to-Practice Model. What every advisor must master.

The program’s methodology, derived from its ten learning goals, six courses and assessment framework. Three layers of competence take a professional from knowing what is true to helping responsibly, inside a clearly defined scope.

Six evidence domains, read through an appraisal lens that separates established evidence from emerging findings and unsupported claims.

  1. 1Explain central biological, behavioral, emotional and social factors associated with longevity and healthy aging.
  2. 2Distinguish between evidence-based longevity practices and claims that lack sufficient scientific support.
  3. 3Evaluate the relationships between nutrition, sleep, stress, behavior, purpose and social connection.
  4. 5Analyze longevity challenges through a multidisciplinary and systems-based perspective.

Built in Stages 1–3, Courses 1–6 · Evidenced by knowledge checks and the portfolio’s evidence summary

The advisory cycle: assess, plan, support and review, practiced in every course through the professional-practice strand.

  1. 7Conduct and document a structured, non-diagnostic longevity and wellbeing assessment.
  2. 8Co-create an individualized plan with measurable goals and an appropriate follow-up process.
  3. 9Apply behavior-change and communication tools in individual or group settings.
  4. 4Apply practical tools for supporting lifelong health and wellbeing.
  5. 6Develop an evidence-informed plan, intervention or educational resource for a defined need.

Built in the practice strand and Stage 4 · Evidenced by applied and case-based assessments, an observed skills assessment and the supervised practice case

Professional boundaries, red flags and referral: the judgment that keeps advisory work safe, ethical and non-clinical.

  1. 10Maintain professional boundaries, recognize red flags and make appropriate referrals.

Built in the orientation and professional-scope lesson, every course and Stage 4 · Evidenced by professional conduct, the portfolio’s risk, ethics and referral analysis and the final oral assessment

07Assessment & certification

Evaluated on competence, not attendance alone.

Achievement is evaluated throughout the program: participation, knowledge checks, applied and case-based assessments, an observed or recorded skills assessment, a supervised practice case and a capstone you present and defend.

Suggested assessment weighting

Five components, one standard

  • Participation, engagement & professional conduct10%
  • Course knowledge checks20%
  • Applied assignments & case-based skills assessments30%
  • Supervised practicum & capstone portfolio30%
  • Final presentation & oral assessment10%

The precise weighting may be adjusted according to Bar-Ilan Akadima’s academic and certification requirements.

Conditions for certification

To receive the professional certificate

  1. Attend at least 85% of synchronous sessions and all required practicum and capstone clinics, unless an approved alternative is completed
  2. Complete all required course assignments
  3. Achieve at least 70% in each course and an overall program grade of at least 75%
  4. Successfully complete the supervised practice case and receive approval for the capstone portfolio
  5. Pass the final presentation and oral assessment
  6. Demonstrate appropriate academic and professional conduct

Participants who attend but do not meet all requirements may receive a certificate of participation, subject to Bar-Ilan Akadima policy, and may not use the graduate designation.

The capstone portfolio

Seven parts, from intake to reflection.

Built progressively throughout the program, the portfolio addresses a clearly defined need, draws on at least four of the six courses, cites credible sources, and documents implementation and follow-up (or an approved simulated equivalent).

  1. iA structured intake and non-diagnostic longevity and wellbeing assessment
  2. iiA clearly defined client or target-population profile and longevity-related need
  3. iiiAn evidence summary drawing on credible sources and content from at least four courses
  4. ivA collaborative, prioritized plan with measurable objectives
  5. vDocumentation of implementation, follow-up, feedback and modifications
  6. viRisks, ethical issues, professional limits and referral considerations
  7. viiA reflective analysis of your decisions, challenges, learning and next steps
